# Unregister Account


This page allows you to **irreversibly** delete your account and the information associated with it: your contacts and your message history on the server. Messages you sent stay with the people who got them. Please read the instructions carefully to fill in the fields correctly.

!!! danger "Warning!"

     The action of deleting a profile is **irreversible**! There is no built-in undo mechanism. Once you tick **I understand that my account is deleted for good** and press the ++"Delete my account"++ button, the action is performed instantly.

## Username

Enter your username, for example `john`. The full address works too: `john@chatrix.one`.

## Password

Enter your current password.
